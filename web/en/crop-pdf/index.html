Crop a PDF – drag the edges.

Remove white margins or a strip you do not want. You see the crop on the first page, and the same measurements are used on every page.

How to crop a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    Drop it on the page.

  2. Drag the edges.

    Drag the yellow handles on the first page, or type the margins in millimetres.

  3. Crop and download.

    Every page is cropped by the same amount.

Common uses

  • Remove white borders around scanned pages.
  • Cut off a header or footer.
  • Let a table or figure fill the page.

Good to know

  • Cropping changes what is shown, not what is in the file. Content outside the edge stays in the file. If you need to remove something for good, use Redact PDF.
  • Every page is cropped with the same margins. Pages that would become too small are skipped.
  • Rotated pages are cropped the right way round, so the edges follow what you see.
  • If you want to remove whole pages instead, use Delete pages.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about cropping PDFs

How do I crop a PDF?

Open the file, drag the edges on the first page or type the margins, and click Crop PDF.

Is the cropped part really removed?

No. Cropping hides the area, but the content stays in the file. For sensitive content, use Redact PDF.

Can I crop different amounts on different pages?

Not in this version. The same margins are used on every page.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
