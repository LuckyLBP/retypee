Merge PDF files – without uploading.

Put several PDFs in the right order and get a single file. Everything happens in your browser, so your documents stay with you.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Choose the files.

    Drop two or more PDF files on the page. You can add more at any time.

  2. Put them in order.

    Drag the files or use the arrows. The top file comes first in the new PDF.

  3. Merge and download.

    Click Merge. You get a new PDF with all the pages in the order you chose.

Common uses

  • Gather an application – CV, cover letter and certificates – in one file.
  • Put an invoice or quote together with its attachments.
  • Merge scanned pages that were saved as separate files.

Good to know

  • Every page of every file is included. If you only want some pages, use Extract pages first.
  • The pages look exactly as before. Bookmarks do not carry over and form fields may stop working.
  • Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged here. Remove the password first.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about merging PDFs

How do I merge PDF files for free?

Drop the files on the page, put them in order and click Merge. The tool is free, needs no account and puts no watermark on the pages.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The merge happens in your browser and the files never leave your computer.

Is there a limit on files or pages?

The tool has no limit of its own. The practical limit is your browser's memory, so very large files can be slow.

Can I merge PDFs on my phone?

Yes, in a modern mobile browser. Pick the files in the file chooser and move them with the arrows.
