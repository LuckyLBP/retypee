Highlight and draw on a PDF – right on the page.

Highlight with a marker, draw freehand and write notes on the pages. Everything you do stays in the saved file, and nothing is uploaded.

How to highlight and draw on a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    All the pages are shown one under the other.

  2. Choose a tool.

    Highlight drags a yellow box, Draw is a pen, Text places a note. Erase removes a mark.

  3. Save.

    The marks are added to a new PDF.

The tools

  • Highlight: drag a box over the text. The colour blends with the text underneath, so it still shows.
  • Draw: freehand drawing in the chosen colour and thickness, good for arrows and circles.
  • Text: click where the note should go and type. Click outside when you are done.
  • Erase: click a mark to remove it. Undo removes the latest one.

Good to know

  • The marks are saved as drawn content on the page and show in every reader. They do not become editable comments.
  • Highlight drags a box and does not follow the lines of the text, so drag over each line.
  • On a touch screen an active tool stops the page from scrolling. Choose Scroll to move around.
  • If you want to remove markup from someone else's file, use Remove comments.
  • If you want to write in a form, use Fill in form.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about highlighting and drawing on PDFs

How do I highlight text in a PDF?

Open the file here, choose Highlight and drag a box over the text. Then click Save to get a new file.

Can I write notes on the page?

Yes. Choose Text, click where the note should go and type.

Can the marks be removed afterwards?

Not in the saved file, since they are drawn onto the page. While you work you can undo or erase them.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
