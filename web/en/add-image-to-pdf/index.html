Add an image to a PDF – drag it into place.

Choose a logo, stamp or picture and drag it where it belongs on the page. You see the result before you save, and everything happens in your browser.

  • No credit card
  • No sign-up
  • Unlimited use

How to add an image to a PDF

  1. Open the PDF and choose the image.

    JPG, PNG, WebP and most other image formats work.

  2. Place the image.

    Drag it on the page, change its size and how see-through it is. Choose whether it goes on every page.

  3. Save.

    The image is added to a new PDF.

Common uses

  • A logo in the corner of every page.
  • A scanned signature or a photo on a form. For a drawn signature there is Sign PDF.
  • A stamp such as APPROVED, or a picture showing who the file comes from.
  • A faint background picture behind the content, with low opacity.

Good to know

  • PNG images with a transparent background keep their transparency.
  • The image sits on top of the page content and becomes part of the file.
  • Large images are reduced to at most 2400 points on the longest side, which is plenty for print.
  • Rotated pages get the image the right way up.
  • If you want to put text across the pages instead, use Watermark.

Questions about adding an image to a PDF

How do I add an image to a PDF?

Open the file here, choose the image and drag it where it belongs. Then click Add the image.

Can I put the same logo on every page?

Yes. Tick the box to put the image on every page and it lands in the same place everywhere.

Does a PNG keep its transparent background?

Yes. Transparent parts of the image let the page underneath show through.

Is the file or the image uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and neither the file nor the image leaves your computer.
