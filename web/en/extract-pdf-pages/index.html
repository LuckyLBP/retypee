Extract pages from a PDF – only the ones you need.

Mark the pages you want to keep and get a new PDF with just those. The rest of the original is left alone.

How to extract pages from a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    The pages are shown as thumbnails.

  2. Mark the pages you want.

    Click the pages, or type page numbers like 1, 3-5.

  3. Extract and download.

    You get a new PDF with the marked pages.

Common uses

  • Send only the relevant part of a long report.
  • Save a receipt or an attachment from a larger PDF.
  • Make an excerpt of a contract.

Good to know

  • The pages end up in the same order as in the original.
  • If you want several parts as separate files, use Split PDF.
  • If you would rather get rid of certain pages, use Delete pages.
  • Password-protected PDFs have to be unlocked first.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about extracting pages

How do I pull a page out of a PDF?

Open the file, click the page you want and click Extract. You get a new PDF with only that page.

Can I pull several pages into one file?

Yes. Mark all the pages you want, for example 1, 3-5, and they end up in the same new PDF.

What is the difference between Extract pages and Split PDF?

Extract pages gives a single new PDF with your chosen pages. Split PDF divides the whole file into several.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
