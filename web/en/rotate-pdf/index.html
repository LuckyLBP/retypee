Rotate a PDF – page by page or the whole file.

Scanned the pages the wrong way round? Turn them right and save a new PDF. You see every page as you turn it.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    The pages are shown as thumbnails.

  2. Turn the pages.

    Use the arrows under each page, or turn them all at once.

  3. Save and download.

    The rotation is written into the new PDF.

Good to know

  • The rotation is saved in the file, so the pages look right in every PDF reader.
  • The text and images are not changed – only how the page is shown.
  • If you want to change the text in the PDF itself, use Edit PDF.
  • Password-protected PDFs have to be unlocked first.

Common uses

  • Fix scanned pages that came out sideways.
  • Turn a single attachment that faces the wrong way.
  • Make a whole document portrait or landscape.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about rotating PDFs

How do I rotate a PDF permanently?

Turn the pages right here and save a new PDF. The rotation is written into the file.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Every page has its own arrows, and you can also turn them all at once.

Does the quality get worse?

No. The pages are not re-encoded – the file just gets a new rotation setting.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
