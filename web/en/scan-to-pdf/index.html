Scan to PDF – photograph paper, get a clean PDF.

Take a photo of each page, or choose pictures you already have. Retypee evens out the light, makes the paper white and gathers the pages in one PDF. The photos stay on your device.

How to scan a document with your phone

  1. Take or choose pictures.

    On a phone the Take a photo button opens the camera. You can also choose pictures from the gallery or the computer.

  2. Choose the look.

    Enhanced removes shadows and makes the paper white, Black and white gives crisp text, and Original keeps the photo. Turn and order the pages.

  3. Create the PDF.

    All pages end up in one PDF in the order you chose.

Tips for a good result

  • Put the paper on a dark, even surface and shoot straight from above.
  • Shoot in good light, but keep your own shadow off the page.
  • Fill the picture with the paper, so the text is larger and sharper.
  • Black and white gives the smallest files and suits text, while Enhanced suits pages with pictures or colour.

How the enhancement works

Each picture is compared with the brightness of the paper nearby. Shadows and uneven light are counted out, and the contrast is raised so the text turns dark and the paper white. It all happens in your browser, and the pictures are sent nowhere.

Good to know

  • The picture is not cropped automatically. If you need to cut away edges, use Crop PDF afterwards.
  • If you want to search the text, run OCR PDF on the result.
  • If you already have clean pictures that should simply become a PDF without any processing, use JPG to PDF.
  • Every picture format the browser can show works, for example JPG, PNG and WebP.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about scanning to PDF

How do I scan a document with my phone?

Open the page on your phone, tap Take a photo and photograph each page. Choose the look and click Create PDF.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It works in the phone's browser and uses the phone's ordinary camera.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The pictures are processed in your browser and never leave your device.

How is this different from JPG to PDF?

Here you can enhance the photos so they look like scans, with white paper and no shadows, or make them black and white. JPG to PDF puts the pictures in as they are.

Can I make the text searchable?

Yes. Create the PDF here and then run it through OCR PDF.
