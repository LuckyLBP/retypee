Remove comments from a PDF – clean up before you share.

Notes, highlights and drawings can reveal more than you meant. Retypee shows which kinds the file has, and you choose what goes.

How to remove comments from a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    Retypee counts the comments and shows them by kind.

  2. Choose what to remove.

    Every kind is ticked. Untick what you want to keep, for example the highlights.

  3. Remove and download.

    You get a new PDF without the chosen comments.

What can be removed

  • Notes and text boxes.
  • Highlights, underlines and strikethroughs.
  • Freehand drawings, lines, arrows and shapes.
  • Stamps, attachments and sound comments.

What is left alone

  • Links in the document.
  • Form fields. If you want to lock them there is Flatten PDF.
  • The ordinary text and images on the pages.

Good to know

  • Comments that have been flattened, baked into the page, are no longer comments and cannot be removed here.
  • If you want to hide text for good, use Redact PDF. It removes the text itself from the file.
  • If you want to remove author and dates too, use Remove metadata.
  • The PDF engine (about 25 MB) is downloaded the first time you use the tool.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about removing comments from PDFs

How do I remove comments from a PDF?

Open the file here, choose which kinds to remove and click Remove. You download a clean copy.

Can I keep the highlights but remove the notes?

Yes. Untick Highlights and leave Notes ticked.

Why are no comments found although I can see highlights?

The highlights are probably baked into the page, for example after flattening, and are no longer comments.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
