PDF to text – get the words out of the document.

Save the text of a PDF as a plain text file or Markdown. The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded.

How to get the text out of a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    Drop it on the page.

  2. Choose a format.

    Plain text keeps the lines as they are in the PDF. Markdown tries to recognise headings and paragraphs.

  3. Get the text.

    You get a file and see the start of the text straight away.

Good for

  • Copying text into another document.
  • Searching or processing the text in another program.
  • Getting the content in a form that screen readers and AI tools handle well.

Good to know

  • Only text that exists in the file is extracted. Scanned pages are images without text, so run OCR first.
  • Markdown headings are guessed from the size of the text, so check the result.
  • Tables become lines of text, not tables.
  • If you want the pages as images instead, use PDF to JPG.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about PDF to text

How do I get the text out of a PDF?

Open the file, choose a format and click Get the text. You download a .txt or .md file.

Why is the text empty?

The pages are probably scanned images. They have no text to extract and need OCR first.

Are tables and images kept?

No. Only the text is extracted. Tables become lines of text and images are left out.

Is the file uploaded?

No. The text is read out in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
