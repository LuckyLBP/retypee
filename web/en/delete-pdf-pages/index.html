Delete pages from a PDF – click and done.

See every page as a thumbnail, mark the ones you want gone and save a new PDF without them. The original file is not changed.

How to remove pages from a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    The pages are shown as thumbnails.

  2. Mark the pages to remove.

    Click a page, or type page numbers like 2, 4-6.

  3. Delete and download.

    You get a new PDF without the marked pages.

Common uses

  • Remove a blank page or a cover sheet.
  • Take out pages that do not belong before you pass the file on.
  • Cut a report down to the pages the recipient needs.

Good to know

  • The new file is built from the pages that stay. The original is left where it was.
  • If you would rather pick the pages to keep, use Extract pages.
  • You cannot delete every page – there would be no PDF left.
  • Password-protected PDFs have to be unlocked first.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about deleting pages

How do I remove a page from a PDF?

Open the file, click the page you want to remove and click Delete. You download a new PDF without that page.

Can I remove several pages at once?

Yes. Mark as many pages as you like, either by clicking or by typing page numbers like 2, 4-6.

Does the original file change?

No. You download a new file and the original stays untouched.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
