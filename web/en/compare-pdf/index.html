Compare two PDFs – see what changed.

Choose the original and the changed version. Retypee shows line by line what was removed and what was added.

How to compare two PDFs

  1. Choose the two files.

    Drop the original on the left and the new version on the right.

  2. Compare.

    The text of both is read out and compared line by line.

  3. Read the changes.

    Removed lines are shown in red and added lines in green, with page numbers. Turn on Show all text to see the whole document.

What is compared

  • The text on the pages, line by line.
  • A page number is shown for every line so you can find the place.

What is not compared

  • Layout, colours and images. Text only.
  • Scanned pages without text. Run OCR first.
  • Files that are completely different. You get a message instead of a long list.

Good to know

  • Nothing is saved or uploaded. The result only exists on the screen.
  • If you changed a single word the whole line is marked.
  • If you want to change the text in a file yourself, use Replace text in PDF.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about comparing PDFs

How do I compare two PDFs?

Drop the original and the changed version on the page and click Compare. You see which lines were removed and added.

Can I compare images and layout?

No, only the text is compared.

Why does it say no text was found?

The pages are probably scanned images without text. They need OCR before they can be compared.

Are the files uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the files never leave your computer.
