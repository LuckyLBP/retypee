Put a watermark on your PDF.

Mark the document as a draft or confidential. You see the stamp on the first page before you save.

How to watermark a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    Drop it on the page.

  2. Type and set up.

    Type the text and choose size, colour, opacity and whether it runs diagonally or straight.

  3. Stamp and download.

    The text is placed in the middle of every page.

Common stamps

  • DRAFT for documents that are not finished.
  • CONFIDENTIAL for sensitive documents.
  • COPY so nobody mistakes a copy for the original.
  • The recipient's name, to show who the file is for.

Good to know

  • The stamp sits on top of the page content and is part of the file. It cannot be switched off in the reader.
  • A watermark is not security. Anyone who wants to can remove it with an editing program.
  • Only characters from the standard character set are supported: letters, digits and ordinary symbols. Emoji do not work.
  • For a small number in the corner instead, use Page numbers.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about watermarking PDFs

How do I put a watermark on a PDF?

Open the file, type the text, choose size and colour and click Add watermark. It lands in the middle of every page.

Can I make the stamp see-through?

Yes. The Opacity slider goes from 5 to 80 percent, so the page underneath shows through.

Can the stamp be removed afterwards?

Not with this tool. Keep the original file if you want to change it or make a new version.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
