OCR for PDF – make scanned pages searchable.

A scanned PDF is just pictures, so you can neither search it nor select the text. Retypee reads the words and lays them invisibly over the page. It looks the same, but the text can be searched and copied.

  • No credit card
  • No sign-up

How to make a scanned PDF searchable

  1. Open the scanned PDF.

    Drop it on the page. Pages that already have text are skipped.

  2. Choose the language.

    English is preselected. The right language gives the best result.

  3. Read and download.

    Each page is read in your browser. You get a new PDF that looks the same but can be searched.

How it works

The pages are drawn as images and read by Tesseract, a well-known OCR engine that runs as WebAssembly right in the browser. The words are then placed as invisible text in exactly the same spot as in the image. The scanned picture stays as it was.

What you can do afterwards

  • Search the document, and select and copy text.
  • Save the text as a file with PDF to text.
  • Make the file smaller with Compress PDF.
  • Compare two versions with Compare PDF.

Good to know

  • The result depends on the scan. Sharp pages with printed text at 200 dpi or more work best, while blurry photos, handwriting and tables with dense layouts do worse.
  • The first time, the OCR engine (about 4 MB) and the language file (2 to 4 MB) are downloaded from a public server. After that they are kept in the browser. Your PDF is never sent.
  • It takes a few seconds per page, and longer on a slow phone.
  • The Cancel button stops the reading and nothing is saved.
  • If you only have photos of paper, start with Scan to PDF.

Questions about OCR for PDFs

How do I make a scanned PDF searchable?

Open the file here, choose the language and click Make searchable. You get a new PDF where the text can be searched, selected and copied.

Is my PDF uploaded when it is read?

No. The reading happens in your browser. Only the OCR engine and the language file are downloaded, never your document.

Which languages are supported?

Swedish, English, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, German, French and Spanish. Choose the language the document is written in.

Why did some words come out wrong?

OCR guesses from the picture. Low resolution, shadows, handwriting and unusual fonts cause more mistakes. A sharper original gives a better result.

Does the look of the pages change?

No. The scanned picture stays as it was and the text sits invisibly on top.
