Add blank pages to a PDF – exactly where you want them.

Mark the pages a blank page should follow, or choose every other page or every page. You see all the pages as thumbnails.

How to add blank pages

  1. Open the PDF.

    The pages are shown as thumbnails.

  2. Mark where.

    Click the pages a blank page should follow, or use the shortcuts for every, odd or even pages.

  3. Create and download.

    You get a new PDF with the blank pages inserted.

Common uses

  • A blank page between chapters, so that each chapter starts on a right-hand page.
  • A blank page after every page, for notes when printing.
  • Padding a document to an even number of pages for double-sided printing or a booklet.
  • A blank page at the start, as a cover to fill in.

Good to know

  • The blank page is as big as the page it follows.
  • You can add a blank page before the first page with its own box.
  • If you want to make a booklet, use Make booklet, which pads with blank pages by itself.
  • If you want to remove blank pages instead, use Remove blank pages.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about adding blank pages to PDFs

How do I insert a blank page in a PDF?

Open the file here, click the page the blank page should follow and click Add. You get a new PDF.

Can I add a blank page after every page?

Yes. Use the shortcut After every page, or After odd pages if you only want them there.

How do I add a blank page at the start?

Tick Also add a blank page first.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
