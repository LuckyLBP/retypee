Sign a PDF – draw or type your signature.

Make a signature, drag it into place and save. The file and your signature stay on your computer.

How to sign a PDF

  1. Make the signature.

    Draw with a mouse or finger, or type your name in a handwriting style.

  2. Place it.

    Drag the signature where it should go, change its size and choose the page.

  3. Sign and download.

    The signature is added to the new PDF.

This is a visible signature

The signature is added as an image on the page. It is like signing on paper and scanning it, not a digital signature with a certificate or an eID. For contracts that require a qualified electronic signature you need a dedicated service.

Good to know

  • The signature is not saved anywhere. You make it again next time.
  • You can put the same signature on every page, for example to initial a document.
  • On a touch screen, drawing with your finger feels the most natural.
  • If you want to fill in text in the document first, use Edit PDF.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about signing PDFs

Is a signature made here legally valid?

It depends on the document. A simple image signature is fine for many everyday agreements, but not where the law requires a qualified electronic signature. Ask the recipient if you are unsure.

Is my signature saved?

No. It only exists in your browser while you work and disappears when you close the tab.

Can I sign several pages?

Yes. Tick the box to put the signature on every page, or sign one page at a time.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and neither the file nor the signature leaves your computer.
