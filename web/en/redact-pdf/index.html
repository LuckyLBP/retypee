Redact a PDF – for real, not just a black box.

Putting a black box over text only hides it on the surface. Retypee removes the text and the image pixels under the box from the file itself.

How to redact a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    The pages are shown at full size.

  2. Mark what should go.

    Draw a box over an area, or search for a word or number and every match is marked. Click a box to remove it.

  3. Redact and check.

    Download the file. Under the boxes the text and image are gone, and a search in the new file shows the words no longer exist.

Why not just draw a black box?

In most PDF editors the black box is a separate layer on top of the page. The text is still there underneath and can be selected, copied or searched. When the text and the pixels under the box are really removed, what was hidden cannot be brought back.

What gets removed

  • Text under the boxes – it is deleted from the page content, not just hidden.
  • Pixels in images that the boxes cover.
  • Metadata (author, app, dates) is cleared too when you download.

Good to know

  • Read the result through before you share it. Names and numbers can appear in several places, so search for them.
  • Text that only exists as an image, such as on a scanned page, is not found by the search. Draw a box over it instead.
  • The editing engine (about 25 MB) is downloaded the first time you use the tool.
  • The tool works best on a computer with a mouse. If you only need to clear the file's hidden details, use Remove metadata.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about redacting PDFs

How do I redact text in a PDF?

Open the file, draw a box over what should go or search for a word, and click Redact. The text under the boxes is removed from the file.

Can what was redacted be recovered?

No. The text and pixels under the boxes are removed from the file, so they cannot be selected, copied or read out. Keep the original separately if you need it yourself.

Can I redact a personal ID number on every page?

Yes. Search for the number and every match is marked. Check that the number is not written in several ways, for example with and without a hyphen.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
