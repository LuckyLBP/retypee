Text to PDF – type, paste, done.

Type or paste text, or drop a text file on the page, and get a PDF with proper line breaks and pages. It all happens in your browser.

How to turn text into a PDF

  1. Type or paste the text.

    Or open a .txt file, or drop it on the page.

  2. Choose the look.

    Font, text size, paper size, margin and whether the pages are numbered.

  3. Create the PDF.

    Lines are broken to fit the width of the page and the text is split into pages.

Good for

  • Notes, letters and drafts that should be sent as a PDF.
  • Logs and code snippets in a fixed-width font.
  • Text from an email or a web page that should be saved.

What you get

Plain text, without formatting such as bold, headings or images. Blank lines, indentation and line breaks are kept. If you need a formatted document a word processor is the right tool, but for text that should become a simple PDF this is quicker.

Good to know

  • Letters such as é, ü and ñ work. Characters that are not in the PDF fonts, for example emoji, are replaced by a question mark and you are told how many.
  • Very long words are broken so they fit on the line.
  • If you want to turn a picture of text into real text, use OCR PDF.
  • If you have several PDFs that should become one, use Merge PDF.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about text to PDF

How do I turn a text file into a PDF?

Open the text file here, or paste the text, choose the look and click Create PDF.

Is the formatting kept?

No. It becomes plain text, with blank lines, indentation and line breaks kept but no bold or headings.

Do accented letters work?

Yes. Letters such as é, ü and ñ work in all three fonts. Emoji and rare symbols are replaced by a question mark.

Is the text uploaded?

No. The PDF is made in your browser and the text never leaves your computer.
