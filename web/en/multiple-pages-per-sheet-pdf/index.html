Multiple pages per sheet – save paper when printing.

Put 2, 4, 6 or 9 pages on each sheet. You see how the sheet is divided while you choose, and the new PDF is made right in your browser.

How to put several pages on one sheet

  1. Open the PDF.

    Drop it on the page.

  2. Choose the layout.

    How many pages per sheet, paper size, direction and whether each page gets a thin border.

  3. Create and download.

    The pages are laid out row by row. You get a new PDF with fewer sheets.

Good for

  • Printing lecture slides, two or six to a sheet.
  • Reading through a long document on little paper.
  • Getting an overview of every page on one sheet.

How the pages are laid out

The pages are placed from left to right, row by row. With automatic direction Retypee chooses a portrait or landscape sheet, and the grid that gives the biggest pages. Two portrait pages therefore end up side by side on a landscape sheet.

Good to know

  • Pages of different sizes are each fitted into their own cell without distortion.
  • Rotated pages are shown the right way up.
  • The text gets smaller, so with 9 pages per sheet small print can be hard to read.
  • If you only need to change the paper size, use Resize pages.
  • If you want to change the order of the pages first, use Organize PDF.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about multiple pages per sheet

How do I print several pages per sheet from a PDF?

Open the file here, choose how many pages you want per sheet and click Create sheets. Then print the new PDF as usual.

Which layouts are there?

2, 4, 6 and 9 pages per sheet, on A4, Letter or A3, portrait or landscape.

Do the pages get distorted?

No. Each page is scaled in proportion and centred in its cell.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
