Compress a PDF – without uploading it.

Big PDFs get stuck in email. Retypee lowers the resolution of the images and tidies the file's structure, and you see how much smaller it got.

How to compress a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    Drop it on the page.

  2. Choose a level.

    Best quality gives a moderate saving. Smallest file lowers the image resolution the most.

  3. Compress and download.

    You see the size before and after. If the file does not get noticeably smaller you are told, instead of getting a pointless file.

What gets smaller

  • Large images get a lower resolution and JPEG quality.
  • Fonts that are only partly used are cut down to the characters needed.
  • The structure of the file is tidied and repacked.

How much you can save

It depends on the content. Scanned documents and PDFs with big photos shrink the most, often to a tenth or less. A PDF that is mostly text is already small and saves only a few percent.

Good to know

  • Images are compressed with some loss. They get less sharp, most on the Smallest file level.
  • Text and vector graphics are not affected.
  • Password-protected PDFs have to be unlocked first, use Unlock PDF.
  • The PDF engine (about 25 MB) is downloaded the first time you use the tool.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about compressing PDFs

How do I compress a PDF without losing quality?

Start with the Best quality level. It only lowers images that are needlessly sharp for screens and email. Text and graphics are not changed.

Why did my PDF hardly get smaller?

It probably consists mostly of text, or its images are already heavily compressed. Retypee tells you instead of handing you a file that is not smaller.

How much can a PDF be compressed?

A scanned PDF can shrink to a tenth or less. An ordinary text file only saves a few percent.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The compression happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
