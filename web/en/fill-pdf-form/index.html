Fill in a PDF form – type straight on the page.

The form's fields become real boxes on top of the page. Type, tick and choose, then save a filled-in PDF. Your details never leave your computer.

How to fill in a PDF form

  1. Open the form.

    Drop the PDF on the page. The fields show up on top of the page.

  2. Fill it in.

    Click a field and type, tick boxes and choose from lists.

  3. Save.

    You get a PDF with your answers. Choose to lock it if the answers should not be changeable.

What works

  • Text fields, including multi-line ones, check boxes, radio buttons and drop-down lists.
  • Forms with several pages. Everything you typed is saved in one file.
  • Text with accented letters such as é, ñ and ü.

Make the answers permanent

By default the form can be filled in again after you have saved it. Tick Make the answers permanent to bake them into the pages, so neither the fields nor the answers can be changed. This suits a form you are about to send in.

Good to know

  • The tool fills in real form fields. If the file has none, such as a scanned paper form, you are told. Then write on the page with Edit PDF, and draw your signature with Sign PDF.
  • Forms built as dynamic XFA forms may show fewer fields than in Adobe.
  • Characters outside the character set, such as emoji, cannot be typed in.
  • Buttons and signature fields in the form are skipped.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about filling in PDF forms

How do I fill in a PDF form?

Open the file here and type straight into the fields shown on the page. When you are done, click Save filled-in PDF.

Why are no fields shown?

The file is probably a scanned paper form without real form fields. Use the editor instead and write with Add text.

Can I make the answers impossible to change?

Yes. Tick Make the answers permanent and the answers are baked into the pages.

Are my file or my details uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and neither the file nor what you type leaves your computer.
