Convert a PDF to grayscale – every colour becomes grey.

Save colour ink when printing, or send a uniform black-and-white copy. The text stays selectable, and nothing is uploaded.

How to make a PDF black and white

  1. Open the PDF.

    Drop it on the page.

  2. Click Make grayscale.

    Every colour in text, images and graphics becomes a shade of grey.

  3. Download.

    You see the size before and after.

What turns grey

  • Coloured text and headings.
  • Photos and other images.
  • Charts, figures and backgrounds.

Grayscale is not pure black and white

The result is shades of grey, like a black-and-white photo, not just black and white. That keeps images and charts readable. If you need a pure black-and-white scan of text, use Scan to PDF and choose Black and white.

Good to know

  • The text stays as text and can be searched and selected.
  • The file often gets smaller, but not always. If you want to shrink it further, use Compress PDF.
  • The colours cannot be brought back. Keep the original if you need it.
  • Password-protected files have to be unlocked first, use Unlock PDF.
  • The PDF engine (about 25 MB) is downloaded the first time you use the tool.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about making a PDF black and white

How do I make a PDF black and white?

Open the file here and click Make grayscale. You get a copy where every colour is a shade of grey.

Does the text disappear?

No. The text stays as text, with grey characters instead of coloured ones.

Does the file get smaller?

Often, because the images have fewer colour channels, but it depends on the content.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
