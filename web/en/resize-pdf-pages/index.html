Resize the pages of a PDF – to A4 or another size.

Mixed page sizes or the wrong paper format? Retypee fits every page onto a sheet of the size you choose, without distorting the content.

How to change the page size of a PDF

  1. Open the PDF.

    You see which sizes the pages have now.

  2. Choose the new size.

    A4, A3, A5, Letter or Legal, and whether the direction of the pages is kept.

  3. Resize and download.

    Each page is scaled to fit and centred on the sheet.

Common uses

  • Turn a US Letter PDF into A4 before printing.
  • Make every page of a merged file the same size.
  • Enlarge an A5 booklet to A4 or the other way round.

How the pages are scaled

Each page is enlarged or reduced so the whole page shows, in proportion, and placed in the middle of the new sheet. If the format differs you get white edges. Nothing is cropped.

Good to know

  • Landscape pages stay landscape unless you choose otherwise.
  • You can add a margin around the content.
  • Text and graphics keep their quality, because the pages are scaled as vectors.
  • If you want to cut away edges instead, use Crop PDF.
  • Several pages on one sheet? Use Pages per sheet.

Your files never leave your device

This tool runs in your browser. The file is read into your device's memory, processed there and saved as a new file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions about resizing PDF pages

How do I turn a PDF into A4?

Open the file here, choose A4 and click Resize pages. Each page is scaled onto an A4 sheet.

Is the content distorted?

No. The proportions are kept, and if the format differs the page gets white edges.

Can I make every page landscape?

Yes. Choose Landscape under Direction, otherwise each page keeps its direction.

Is the file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and the file never leaves your computer.
